Welcome to YTKTV

YTKTV turns a computer into a karaoke (KTV) machine for a home or a karaoke room.

What it does
- Sing any YouTube video, or your own song files, with no ads.
- Removes the vocals with AI, so you sing over the band; switch between original and instrumental any time.
- Lyrics on the screen, timed to the song; with a graphics card they are written from the song itself.
- Scores every song, with confetti for a great one.
- Four microphones with a mixer: echo, reverb, EQ, voice effects and feedback protection.
- Phones as remotes: scan the QR code to choose songs, see the queue and control playback.
- Party games, mood scenes for the lights, favourites and history.
- Works with YTKTV Desk, the front desk app for karaoke venues.

What you need
- macOS 15 or later, Windows 10 (1809) or 11, or a 64-bit Linux (also Raspberry Pi 4/5).
- About 1.5 GB of free disk space, and more for the songs it keeps for later.
- mpv to play the songs. If it is missing, this installer (or YTKTV, when it first opens) offers to install it.
- A graphics card (GPU) is optional: vocal removal and AI lyrics are faster with one.
- A second display or TV is optional: the MV goes full screen there and the main screen keeps the song list.

Where it goes
- macOS: /Applications/YTKTV.app. Windows: your Programs folder. Linux: ~/Applications/YTKTV.AppImage.
- Settings and the song cache stay in your user folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

Updates
- YTKTV checks for updates by itself. Small updates download only the files that changed.

Privacy
- No account and no tracking. YTKTV talks to YouTube for songs, to GitHub for updates, and to the
  phones and the Desk on your own network.
