Welcome to YTKTV Desk

YTKTV Desk runs the front desk of a karaoke venue whose rooms use YTKTV.

What it does
- Opens and closes the rooms, with timers, extensions and packages.
- Bills each room: time, food and drinks, coupons and members' balances; prints receipts.
- Takes bookings and keeps a waiting list.
- Members with points, top-ups and birthdays.
- Orders and service calls from the rooms, on the Desk and on staff terminals.
- Reports and insights: takings, busy hours, top songs and best sellers.
- A lobby TV showing which rooms are free, in use or booked (never what a room sings).
- Server Mode: a computer with a graphics card removes vocals and writes lyrics for every room.

What you need
- macOS 15 or later, Windows 10 (1809) or 11, or a 64-bit Linux.
- About 500 MB of free disk space.
- The Desk and the rooms on the same network. Each room runs YTKTV and pairs with a code from the Desk.

Where it goes
- macOS: /Applications/YTKTV Desk.app. Windows: your Programs folder. Linux: ~/Applications/YTKTV-Desk.AppImage.
- The venue's records stay in your user folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them. Back them up from the Desk's settings.

Updates
- YTKTV Desk checks for updates by itself. Small updates download only the files that changed.
