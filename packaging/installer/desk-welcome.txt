Welcome to YTKTV Desk

YTKTV Desk runs a karaoke venue's front desk: it opens and closes the rooms running YTKTV, takes orders, bookings and members, prints receipts and reports, and can show the rooms on a lobby TV.

Before you start
- Install YTKTV Desk on the front desk computer, and YTKTV on each room's computer. Rooms pair with the Desk on the same network.
- With a graphics card (GPU), the Desk can remove vocals and write lyrics for every room.

YTKTV Desk is free to use and must not be sold. Read the license on the next page.
