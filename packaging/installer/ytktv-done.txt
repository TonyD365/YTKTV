YTKTV is installed.

- Open YTKTV from Applications (macOS) or the Start menu (Windows).
- Scan the QR code on the screen with a phone to choose songs and use it as a remote.
- YTKTV updates itself. Small updates download only the files that changed.

Help and source code: https://github.com/TonyD365/YTKTV
Report a problem: https://github.com/TonyD365/YTKTV/issues
