YTKTV is installed.

Getting started
- Open YTKTV from Applications (macOS), the Start menu (Windows), or run the AppImage (Linux).
- Search for a song, or add a folder of your own songs in Settings.
- Scan the QR code on the screen with a phone to choose songs and use it as a remote.
- Plug in microphones and set them up on the Mixer page.
- With a second display, the MV goes full screen there.
