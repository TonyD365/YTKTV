YTKTV Desk is installed.

- Open YTKTV Desk from Applications (macOS) or the Start menu (Windows).
- To add a room, open YTKTV on the room's computer and enter the pairing code shown by the Desk.
- YTKTV Desk updates itself. Small updates download only the files that changed.

Help and source code: https://github.com/TonyD365/YTKTV
Report a problem: https://github.com/TonyD365/YTKTV/issues
