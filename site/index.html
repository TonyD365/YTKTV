Questions

Is it free? Can I use it in my bar?

Yes and yes. YTKTV is free and its license allows using it in a business, such as a karaoke venue. It does not allow selling the software itself. Playing music in public usually also needs licences from the rights holders.

My voice comes out of the speakers late.

The speakers are almost certainly wireless: AirPlay adds one to two seconds and Bluetooth a few hundred milliseconds, and no setting can change that. Connect the speakers or TV with a cable and choose them under Mixer → MAIN OUT.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. Apple Silicon Macs use their GPU, and on Windows and Linux YTKTV uses an NVIDIA or DirectX 12 card when there is one and the processor otherwise.

Does it need the internet?

Yes, the songs come from YouTube. The next songs in the queue are downloaded ahead, so a short drop usually goes unnoticed.

Something does not work.

Please open an issue with the version, your system and what happened.
