# Code of conduct

YTKTV is about getting people to sing together. The same spirit applies here.

## Be kind

- Be welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, language, background or identity.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge. Many people here write in their second language.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people. Explain what you would do instead.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexual content, or publishing anyone's private details.

## Where this applies

In issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki and anywhere else you represent the project.

## If something is wrong

Tell the maintainer privately, through the contact on
[TonyD365's GitHub profile](https://github.com/TonyD365). Reports are kept confidential.
The maintainer may edit or remove content and, in serious or repeated cases, block people from
the project.
