# Getting help

1. **Read the guide.** The [user guide](docs/guide/README.md) covers setting up, the mixing
   console, phones, and the most common problems. The [FAQ](README.md#faq) answers the
   questions people ask most.
2. **Check the known problems** in [Troubleshooting](docs/guide/troubleshooting.md).
3. **Search the [issues](https://github.com/TonyD365/YTKTV/issues?q=is%3Aissue)**: someone may
   have had the same problem.
4. **Open an issue** with the [form](https://github.com/TonyD365/YTKTV/issues/new/choose). The
   version, your system and what happened make it much quicker to help. English or Chinese are
   both fine.

Security problems are different: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
